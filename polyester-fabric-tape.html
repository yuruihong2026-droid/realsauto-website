FAQ

LY6524 Polyester Fabric Tape common questions

What temperature range does LY6524 cover?

-40°C to +125°C, suitable for general harness bundling in medium-temperature environments.

Is LY6524 suitable for high-abrasion areas?

It provides moderate abrasion resistance. For heavy-duty abrasion, consider aluminum foil or thicker constructions.

What width and length options are available?

Standard widths of 15, 19 and 25 mm in 25 m rolls. Custom options available on request.

Can I request a sample?
