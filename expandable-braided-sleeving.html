FAQ

Protective Sleeving common questions

What is the LY3400 self-wrap sleeve?

A woven polyester sleeve with a self-wrapping open design for easy installation and re-opening.

What temperature range does sleeving cover?

-40°C to +150°C with excellent abrasion resistance.

What inner diameters are available?

Standard inner diameters of 6, 10, 16 and 22 mm in 10 m rolls.

Can the sleeve be opened after installation?

Yes, the self-wrapping design allows re-opening for service and maintenance.
