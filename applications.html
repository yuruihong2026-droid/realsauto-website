A specification-led supply route

LY6512 PVC, LY6531 fleece, LY6524 fabric, LY6525 heavy fabric, and LY6632 aluminium foil are manufactured by our partner plant to REALS specification. Tell us the working zone first, then share the temperature requirement, contact surfaces, movement, noise target, colour need, and requested documentation. We can then identify which current grade should enter your own validation process and provide the available technical data for review.
