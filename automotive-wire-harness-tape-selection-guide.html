Procurement & engineering guide

How to select automotive wire harness protection materials.

Start with the application environment, then compare noise, abrasion, temperature, installation and harness-fixing requirements. This guide helps purchasing and engineering teams prepare a focused first technical enquiry.

Step 1 / Define the application

What does the harness need to withstand?

Interior routing

Noise and movement

For interior areas where squeak and rattle matter, identify the contact surfaces, motion points and target noise-damping requirement.

Step 2 / Build a clear enquiry

Six details that speed up the first technical review.

01

Harness location

Interior, body, door, roof, engine compartment, battery or another application area.

02

Operating conditions

Expected temperature, moisture, fluid exposure, vibration and service environment.

03

Protection target

Noise reduction, abrasion resistance, flexible bundling, thermal shielding or electrical insulation.

04

Supply specification

Current material, width, roll length, color, packaging and estimated annual demand.
