Automotive application guide

Automotive Wire Harness Wrapping

Automotive wire harness tape should be chosen by the zone, contact points, temperature, and function of the routed bundle.

Home/Applications/Automotive harness

A vehicle harness runs through the instrument panel, doors, chassis, and engine bay. Each area places different demands on the wrapping material: cabin branches may prioritise soft contact and low noise, door or grommet routes face repeated abrasion, and engine-bay bundles require greater heat capability. One tape is therefore not the automatic answer for every branch.

Tape also follows branches, bends, breakouts, and irregular connector transitions. It can wrap almost any harness shape while maintaining overlap around the bundle. A sleeve must normally be fitted over an accessible straight section, so tape is often the more adaptable choice where geometry changes or a completed branch cannot be passed through a tube.
