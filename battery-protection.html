Battery Protection Materials for Cell and Pack Manufacturing

REALS develops sourcing and custom converting solutions for battery electrical insulation, cell cushioning and pack sealing. PET and PI films, PU and silicone foams, and gasket constructions are evaluated against each customer drawing and performance requirements. Availability and claims are subject to sample testing and project approval.
