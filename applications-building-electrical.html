Electrical application guide

Building & Electrical Conduit

Electrical insulation tape for wiring should be selected around the installation task, required documentation, temperature, colour, and substrate.

Home/Applications/Building electrical

Building wiring, conduit work, and control cabinets involve several distinct tape tasks. Installers may need general insulation around wiring, clear visual identification inside a panel, organised bundling, or a metallic tape for shielding and jointing. The same roll should not be assumed suitable for every task; begin with the function and documentation required for the project.
