Appliance application guide

Appliance & White Goods Harness

Appliance wire harness tape helps organise internal wiring where compact routing, movement, vibration, and assembly handling meet.

Home/Applications/Appliance harness

White goods and small appliances contain internal wiring that must remain organised through assembly and service. Tape may be used for bundling, electrical insulation, noise reduction, or abrasion protection where a harness approaches a door, hinge, cabinet edge, compressor, fan, or other vibrating part. The working location should determine the construction evaluated.
